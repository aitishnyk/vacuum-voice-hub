## Actual inventory and limitations

- **223** source-attributed model profiles. New entries are identity/research profiles unless exact firmware/device evidence separately exists. All legacy model IDs/aliases remain preserved and guarded by CI.
- **55** attributed voice variants; source attribution does not by itself authorize audio redistribution, and does not indicate that all media bytes are bundled in Git.
- **22** reference text-script locales (16 common phrases each). New languages require native-speaker proofreading; they are not 22 prerecorded languages.
- **12,265** 223 × 55 software/research target combinations, not physically accepted installations.
- **Xiaomi X10 `dreame.vacuum.r2209` is the ONLY physically verified VVH custom install target.** Other transports remain experimentally scoped, vendor-signed-only or build/preview-only; no signature bypass.
- Creator Studio, source audio QA, Human Review, Ed25519 whole-pack signing, offline individual/batch mastering, SHA-256 manifests, per-model research and public catalog are available in the offline stable source.
- Community exact-model+firmware testing and optional hardware loans/donations from manufacturers/users are welcomed, without an obligation to have every device in our lab or compromise test independence.
- No Telegram bots, Stars monetization, Bunny commercial media storage or proprietary firmware is included in this public source.

**SOURCE SEALED** is not a notarized desktop binary, official GitHub Release tag/asset or manufacturer acceptance. Physical testing and software maintenance continue independently.
