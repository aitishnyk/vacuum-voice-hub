## Community archives / discovery sources

Many historic packs originated on **4PDA**, Mindsolo-era collections, VK groups and old Roborock/Xiaomi communities. Some original authors are no longer identifiable. VVH records the best-known provenance in `catalog/voices.json` and `catalog/research-backlog.json`.

Relevant research threads include:

- https://4pda.to/forum/index.php?showtopic=881982
- https://4pda.to/forum/index.php?showtopic=1012688
- https://4pda.to/forum/index.php?showtopic=1024479

If you created one of the listed packs and want your name/link corrected, added, or removed, open an issue. Attribution fixes are treated as high priority.
