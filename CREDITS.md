## Historic Russian Dreame discovery metadata

Old pack manifests were preserved from:
- **fidlow/fidlow.github.io — soundpackage.json**
  https://github.com/fidlow/fidlow.github.io
- **Maxmudjon/Get_MiHome_devices_token**
  https://github.com/Maxmudjon/Get_MiHome_devices_token

These manifests retain names, historical URLs, MD5 and sizes for packs such as Bender+Alice, StarCraft 2, Yeltsin, Tatyana, Filip, Svarlivaya Zhena and others. VVH keeps them in the **recovery backlog**, not the installable catalog, until the actual artifact is independently recovered and verified.

## Community archives / discovery sources

Many historic packs originated on **4PDA**, Mindsolo-era collections, VK groups and old Roborock/Xiaomi communities. Some original authors are no longer identifiable.

- https://4pda.to/forum/index.php?showtopic=881982
- https://4pda.to/forum/index.php?showtopic=1012688
- https://4pda.to/forum/index.php?showtopic=1024479

If you created one of the listed packs and want your name/link corrected, added, or removed, open a credit-correction issue. Attribution fixes and legitimate removal requests are high priority.
